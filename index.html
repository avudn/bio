I am interested in source-based Linux operating systems.

The thing I love in them, is how you can make them feel and look whatever you want it to feel and look like, and yet, they stay lightweight, thanks to "use" flags and large amount of customization. But generally, the thing I like most in Linux, is it's minimalism, and maximalism at the same time, the amount of customization with the Linux kernel & many distributions allows you to make your system look however you want. That is why I chose Linux in general.

Linux distributions that I use, and why:

Gentoo Linux


Gentoo Linux was the best discovery in my Linux journey. This is a distribution which made me realize, that I really love the concept of source-based distributions, and crazy amount of customization. The first installation process taught me many things about how linux works internally, and after installing it, the learning process went so much faster. P.S. I used CachyOS for a week as my first distribution, before switching to Gentoo. So basically everything was new to me, which allowed me to explore and learn things.

Exherbo Linux


I've been interested in Exherbo Linux for a pretty long time, because everyone said that it's "masochistic", "unusable", and such. After trying it for the first time, I REALLY loved the amount of DIY in it. It's more manual than Gentoo, and that lets you explore Linux even more. Currently, I am dualbooting it with my main Gentoo.
